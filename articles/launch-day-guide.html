Launch week guide

GTA 6 launch day guide

Updated 16 November 2026. Everything here is as of 16 November 2026, three days before launch.

GTA 6 unlocks on Thursday 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar Support says that happens at midnight local time in every region, so New Zealand gets it first. If you have pre-ordered digitally, the pre-load is already open and needs up to 212 GB of free space, so the most useful thing you can do before launch night is get it downloaded.

Quick answers

  • When: midnight local time on Thursday 19 November 2026, according to Rockstar Support. New Zealand unlocks first.
  • US West Coast: outlets disagree. US store listings point to one US-wide time of midnight Eastern, which is 9pm PT on Wednesday 18 November. Check your console's store page.
  • Pre-load: open now for digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It needs up to 212 GB, depending on your device.
  • Physical copies: there's no disc. The box holds a download code, so you still download the whole game.
  • Price: $79.99 Standard and $99.99 Ultimate in the US; £69.99 and £89.99 in the UK.
  • Frame rate: 30 fps on every console. The PS5 Pro adds PSSR and ray-traced shadows.
  • Free map: a printable map of Leonida and a cover art poster go up as free downloads on Rockstar's site on 19 November.
  • No PC version and no PS4 version at launch. Xbox One players can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming.

Unlock times by region

Rockstar's support page, updated on 3 November, says GTA 6 releases at midnight local time on 19 November in all regions, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Because local midnight arrives in New Zealand before anywhere else, Kiwi players are first in the world. That happens at 11am UK time on Wednesday 18 November.

Pre-loading doesn't let you start early. The files sit on your console, locked, until the unlock time arrives.

The US question: one time for the whole country?

Read literally, "midnight local time" would mean midnight in each US time zone. But according to Forbes and Vice, the US PlayStation Store listing carries a single timestamp for the whole country: 05:00 UTC, which is midnight Eastern. If that holds, the game unlocks at 11pm Central, 10pm Mountain and 9pm Pacific on Wednesday 18 November. GameRant's pre-load table, published the day before pre-loading opened, also listed 9pm PT for the US West Coast on PS5, though the same article says PS5 pre-loads open at local midnight.

Outlets disagree, though, and a few guides still say each US time zone gets its own midnight. Rockstar hasn't addressed it, and no official source gives a US Xbox unlock time. Check the release time on your own console's store page.

Australia: Sydney and Brisbane

Sydney and Melbourne unlock at midnight local time, which matches the PlayStation Store listing reported by Vice. Brisbane isn't settled. Queensland has no daylight saving, so in November it's an hour behind Sydney. If Australian stores use one Sydney-based time for the whole country, as the US store seems to with Eastern time, Brisbane could unlock at 11pm on Wednesday 18 November rather than at its own midnight. We haven't found a source that settles it, so check your store page.

What about the "New Zealand trick"?

Some outlets have written about switching an Xbox's region to New Zealand to play earlier. Vice says it hasn't been confirmed to work, and that according to Sony a PSN account's country can't be changed and changing the console clock doesn't help. We wouldn't bother.

Pre-load and storage

Pre-loading has been open since Tuesday 10 November for everyone who pre-ordered digitally. It started at different moments on each console:

  • PS5: midnight local time on 10 November, in each time zone.
  • Xbox Series X|S: once, worldwide, at midnight GMT on 10 November. That was 7pm ET on Monday 9 November in the US, and late morning on 10 November in Australia.

Rockstar first said pre-loading would start on 12 November, then moved it up to 10 November in a 3 November update. If a guide still says 12 November, it's out of date.

How much space you need

Rockstar's wording is that the pre-load needs up to 212 GB, depending on your device. PlayStation's own GTA 6 page tells you to clear at least 212 GB. Rockstar hasn't published separate sizes for PS5, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S, so plan for the full figure. GameRant suggests leaving an extra 15 to 25 GB on top for unpacking and patches. That's outlet advice, not Rockstar's, but it's sensible.

Getting the download going

If your console has automatic downloads switched on, the pre-load should already have started by itself. If not, outlets such as KeenGamer give these steps:

  • PS5: go to your Game Library, pick GTA 6 and choose Download, then leave the console on or in Rest Mode.
  • Xbox: go to My games & apps, then Full library, pick the game and choose Install, then leave the console on or in Sleep mode.

On PS5, Sony's own support pages explain how to keep downloads running in Rest Mode. Under Settings, then System, then Power Saving, turn on Stay Connected to the Internet in the features available in rest mode. Then turn on Auto-Download in your Automatic Updates settings.

Clearing space

  • PS5 USB drives are for storage, not play. According to Sony, PS5 games can't run from USB extended storage or download straight to it. You can move other PS5 games there to free up internal space, and copying them back later is faster than downloading them again. Sony lists drives of 5 Gbps or faster, 250 GB to 8 TB, plugged in directly rather than through a hub.
  • A PS5 M.2 SSD adds playable space. Sony's storage page lists the options, and the PlayStation GTA 6 page links to it.
  • Xbox Series X|S: Microsoft says games optimised for Series X|S must be installed on the internal SSD or a Storage Expansion Card.

The 1.002 update

An update numbered 1.002 started rolling out on 13 November. According to KeenGamer, it's about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.18 GB on Xbox; Vice reported around 1 GB on PS5. Rockstar hasn't called it the day-one patch and hasn't published patch notes. Either way, get it installed before launch night.

Physical copies: a code in a box

If you're buying GTA 6 in a shop, know this first: there's no disc. Rockstar's pre-order notes say plainly that a disc won't be in the box. Instead you get a code to redeem on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store, and then the game downloads like any digital copy.

  • You still download everything. That's up to 212 GB, so you need an internet connection and a PlayStation or Xbox account.
  • Boxes have been in shops since 12 November. Rockstar said code-in-box copies for both consoles should be on sale from that date, and once you redeem the code you can pre-load too.
  • Region matters on PlayStation. Rockstar Support says PlayStation codes are region-locked and Xbox codes aren't. Japanese PlayStation codes expire 170 days after launch.
  • What's inside: an unboxing reported by PC Guide shows the download code and a poster. There's no paper map in the box.

Rockstar hasn't announced a disc version. Talk of a later disc came from a Rockstar Support email, but VGC reported that it referred to the code-in-box version.

What the Ultimate Edition adds

Rockstar says the Ultimate items unlock chapter by chapter through Jason and Lucia's story. They include a '95 Grotti Cheetah, a '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy with its own garage, a Classic Car Collection, Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers, personalised pistols for Jason and Lucia, Jason's safehouse vehicles, and extra shops such as the Electric Fang Tattoo parlour (50+ tattoos by FAILE), Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's.

Pre-order bonuses and deadlines

Vintage Vice City Pack

Every pre-order gets the Vintage Vice City Pack. It includes a '55 Vapid Stanier and a Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach (with a weapon locker and somewhere to fence goods), outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a tropical weapon pattern based on Tommy Vercetti's famous shirt. Rockstar says it covers pre-orders and purchases made before 20 November. For physical pre-orders, it's while supplies last.

A free month of GTA+

Digital pre-orders on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store come with one free month of GTA+, one per account. It renews at the paid price unless you cancel it, so make a note if you don't want to keep it. The two stores word the deadlines differently:

The free Leonida map

On 19 November, Rockstar is putting two free digital downloads on its official site at rockstargames.com/VI: a printable cover art poster and a map of Leonida. Rockstar hasn't said exactly what time they'll appear or which page they'll sit on, so check rockstargames.com/VI on launch day.

If you'd rather have it printed, a double-sided poster and map will be sold through the Rockstar Store "shortly after launch" for $0.99, or free with any other Rockstar Store order. Remember that the physical game box doesn't include a map.

Performance and PS5 Pro

Rockstar confirmed on 10 November that GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. Its post mentions no 60 fps mode, and Rockstar says the game was built from the ground up for current-generation consoles.

  • PS5 Pro: the same 30 fps target, plus PSSR (Sony's upscaling) for a clearer image, and ray-traced shadows that only the Pro gets. The PlayStation listing carries a "PS5 Pro Enhanced" badge. Rockstar hasn't given any resolution figures.
  • Base PS5: DualSense haptics throughout (adjustable in the accessibility settings), adaptive triggers, the controller speaker, Tempest 3D Audio and faster loading.

Talk of a 40 or 60 fps mode on the Pro comes from analysts and rumour accounts, as TechRadar covers. Rockstar hasn't announced either.

Accessibility: Rockstar hasn't published a full list of accessibility options yet. The only one confirmed so far is the adjustable haptics setting.

Cloud play and Remote Play

Xbox Cloud Gaming

GTA 6 can be streamed through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and on Xbox One, which is the only way to play it on an Xbox One. Microsoft says streaming begins at 1am ET on 19 November (6am UK time), so it starts an hour after the US East Coast console unlock.

  • Streams use Xbox Series S graphics settings, and Rockstar warns that connection speed may affect performance.
  • You need to own the game; it isn't in Game Pass. Cloud playtime can be bought without Game Pass in supported markets.
  • There's no cloud play on PC at launch, and no PC version.

Remote Play

Rockstar says GTA 6 fully supports Remote Play on both PlayStation and Xbox, so you can stream from your own console to another screen in your home.

Launch week events

  • Wednesday 18 November: "A Night in Vice City". The Miami Heat play the Milwaukee Bucks as "Vice City" at the Kaseya Center, on a custom court and in reimagined kits. It was announced on 2 October as a Heat and Rockstar partnership.
  • Thursday 19 November: launch day. The game unlocks, the free map and poster go up, and Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album comes out with 34 original tracks made with Atlantic Records. Limited-edition black and white GTA 6 DualSense controllers also go on sale from PlayStation Direct and retailers ($84.99, €84.99 or £74.99). There's no official GTA 6 console bundle, though some retailers sell their own.
  • Saturday 21 November: Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival. Rockstar and PlayStation are holding it at Mana Wynwood in Miami, with two stages produced by the III Points team. Headliners include Travis Scott, Cardi B, Metro Boomin and a Justice DJ set. Tickets were lottery only and entries closed on 10 November, so it's winners only now.

No big retailer, including GameStop, GAME, Smyths, EB Games or JB Hi-Fi, has announced a midnight opening. With boxes on sale since 12 November, there's little reason to queue.

Launch-night checklist

  • Check your pre-load is finished. Look in your game library and make sure the download says complete, not queued or paused.
  • Install the 1.002 update. It's small, but you don't want to be downloading it at midnight.
  • Keep space spare. Up to 212 GB for the game, plus some room for patches.
  • Leave the console on or in Rest Mode or Sleep. On PS5, check that Stay Connected to the Internet is switched on.
  • Physical copy? Redeem the code now, so the download is done before launch night.
  • Check your unlock time on your console's store page, especially in the western US or Brisbane.
  • Sign in to PSN or Xbox network ahead of time, in case of a rush at midnight.
  • Claim your GTA+ month if you pre-ordered digitally, and set a reminder if you don't want it to renew.
  • Grab the free map from rockstargames.com/VI on 19 November.

Lessons from past Rockstar launches

These are past events, not predictions about GTA 6. But they show the kinds of things that have gone wrong before.

GTA V (September 2013)

On Xbox 360, GTA V came on two discs: an install disc with about 8 GB of required data, and a play disc. Rockstar Support advised against installing the play disc as well, because that caused late texture pop-in, and some older consoles crashed or struggled to read the disc.

GTA Online (October 2013)

GTA Online went live two weeks after GTA V. Players ran into "Rockstar Cloud Servers are unavailable" errors and connection problems, and Rockstar fixed things with title updates over the following weeks.

Red Dead Redemption 2 (October 2018)

RDR2 had a launch-day title update of about 3.3 GB on PS4, listed on Rockstar Support. A later update, 1.03, switched on the Red Dead Online beta, which rolled out in stages, with Ultimate Edition owners first. According to Windows Central and GamesBeat, that beta had "Coming Soon" screens on Xbox, crashes and connection trouble.

What that means for this week

Rockstar launches tend to come with day-one updates, and their online modes have had rough first days. GTA 6 is single-player at launch, so our guess is that any hiccups will come from busy stores, downloads and patches rather than online servers. That's our expectation, not a fact, and it's why downloading early is the best thing you can do.
