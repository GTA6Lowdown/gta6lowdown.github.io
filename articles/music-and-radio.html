Music and radio

GTA 6 MUSIC AND RADIO GUIDE

Updated as of 16 November 2026, three days before launch.

Rockstar has announced six GTA 6 radio stations so far, from modern Latin on Cocoteo FM to Slayer-hosted metal on The Chamber 106.6, and says there are more to come. On top of the radio there's a 34-track official album out on 19 November, an original score by Justice, and a one-night festival in Miami on 21 November.

Here's every station with its genre, hosts and the songs Rockstar previewed, plus the trailer songs, both albums, the festival and the Daft Punk story that went round in October.

Quick answers

  • How many radio stations? Six have been announced: Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics. Rockstar hasn't given a total.
  • Who hosts them? Rockstar credits stage names. Press coverage of the 8 October announcement widely reported the people behind them, including Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen, Burna Boy, Robyn, Slayer's Kerry King and Tom Araya, and Trick Daddy and Trina.
  • Can you listen on foot? Yes. Radio and podcasts play through headphones on Jason's or Lucia's phone.
  • Are there podcasts? Yes, on-demand podcasts are a first for the series. No titles or hosts have been announced yet.
  • When is the album out? Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (34 tracks, Atlantic Records) is out on 19 November 2026, launch day.
  • Who wrote the score? Justice. It plays in the game from launch; the score album follows on 11 December 2026.
  • The festival? Saturday 21 November 2026 at Mana Wynwood, Miami. Tickets went by lottery, which closed on 10 November.
  • Did Daft Punk turn GTA 6 down? That claim comes from one X post and hasn't been verified. A former Daft Punk member did produce a song on The Album.

Every announced radio station

Rockstar revealed the first six stations on 8 October 2026, with a short audio preview for each one. Every preview credited three songs, so 18 tracks have been officially shown so far. Nothing new has been added since, and as of 16 November Rockstar still hasn't said how many stations the game has in total.

There are clearly more on the way. Rockstar's music page describes the selection as running "from Classic Rock to Talk Radio", and none of the six announced stations is a classic rock or talk station. It also calls this its biggest and widest-ranging mix of radio and podcasts yet. For comparison, GTA IV and GTA V each launched with somewhere around 17 to 19 stations, depending on how you count talk radio, but that's only a guide, not a promise.

A small naming note: Rockstar's music page uses the short labels "Cocoteo", "Back Country", "Afro Bank FM" and "The Chamber" on its station logos. Rockstar's 8 October announcement, and most coverage, uses Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM and The Chamber 106.6. We use the longer forms here.

Cocoteo FM

The Latin station covers today's sound, from bachata and merengue to dembow and reggaeton. Rockstar pitches it as a party that never switches off. It suits a game set in a version of Miami, and Bad Bunny's "EoO" is one of the three preview songs.

Back Country Radio

This is the country station for the swampier, rural side of Leonida: the Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn and the roads in between. Rockstar calls it airboat-ready renegade country. Alongside the two main hosts there's Mud Dog Mike, a swamp weatherman and roving reporter who calls in from an airboat, and who Rockstar jokingly describes as Delta's not-so-secret lover. The previews lean old-school outlaw: David Allan Coe, Loretta Lynn, and Hank Williams Jr. with Waylon Jennings.

AfroBank FM

AfroBank FM covers African music from 20th-century classics and undiscovered gems to current amapiano and South Africa's 3-step sound. The previews include Ghanaian highlife greats Pat Thomas and Ebo Taylor ("Gye Wan"), and Asake's "Joha" brings modern Afrobeats into the mix.

The Chamber 106.6

The metal station promises decades of metal anthems; the three previews run from 1986 to 1994, according to Loudwire. The preview trio is about as heavy as GTA radio has ever been: Black Sabbath, Pantera and Slayer's "Raining Blood".

Flash FM

Flash FM is the pop station, and a returning name: it first appeared in GTA: Vice City in 2002, and Rockstar calls it a Vice City institution. This time the previews are modern, with Charli xcx, MGMT, and Zara Larsson with PinkPantheress.

Dirty South Classics

The rap station focuses on regional Southern hip-hop classics. With Miami's own Trick Daddy turning up on DJ Khaled's "Born N Raised", it's the most local-sounding of the six.

Who the hosts are

On its music page, Rockstar credits every host by a character or stage name: El Martillo and La Gata, MW and Delta Dawne, Burna and Palmsy, DJ Kerry and DJ Tom, Robyn and Alex, and Trick and Trina. The real people behind those names come from wide press reporting on the day of the announcement, with Consequence, Stereogum, Rolling Stone, Dexerto, DJ Mag, RouteNote and others all naming the same artists. The clearest hint from an artist's own side came from Lana Del Rey, who teased the announcement on her Instagram Story the day before, according to Stereogum.

Radio and podcasts on foot

Two things change how music works in GTA 6, both from Rockstar's 8 October announcement.

  • You can listen on foot. Radio no longer stops when you get out of the car. Jason and Lucia's smartphones now support headphones and earbuds, so you can keep a station playing while you walk around, as well as in vehicles.
  • On-demand podcasts. GTA has always had talk radio, but this is the first time the series has podcasts you can pick and play on demand. Rockstar hasn't announced any podcast titles or hosts yet, according to Game Informer and Dexerto.

In practice that should make the radio feel more like a phone full of music than a car stereo. How you pick stations and podcasts on foot is something we'll only see properly once the game is out.

The songs in the Extended Look

Rockstar's 26-minute preview, Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look, streamed on Netflix on 27 August 2026 before landing on Rockstar's YouTube channel. It used licensed songs, but Rockstar didn't publish a tracklist and no station was named on screen. Outlets identified the songs by ear: Mixmag Asia listed 14 and RockstarINTEL 15, among them Phil Collins' "Against All Odds", Depeche Mode's "People Are People", Def Leppard's "Love Bites" and Kodak Black's "Skrilla". Treat that list as a good guess at what's in the game, not an official one.

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album

Rockstar and Atlantic Records announced Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on 17 September 2026. It's a collection of 34 original tracks, out on 19 November 2026, the same day as the game. A few shop listings show 20 November, but Rockstar's own date is 19 November.

  • Label: Atlantic Records, part of Warner Music Group.
  • Formats: digital, a double vinyl LP and CD. Atlantic has its own exclusives, including a liquid-filled vinyl, a splatter vinyl and a CD jewel case, and each retailer has its own vinyl design with a poster, according to Rockstar's music page and Music Business Worldwide.
  • Tracklist: ten singles are out (below). The full tracklist hasn't been officially announced yet.

The Justice score

The French duo Justice (Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay) composed GTA 6's original score. It's a dynamic score, meaning it adapts to what's happening in the game, and it plays in the game from launch. Rockstar named Justice as the composers on 15 October 2026 alongside the Launch Trailer, and announced the score album on 29 October.

In an interview covered by EDM.com, de Rosnay said the size of the game meant the music had to stretch very wide, and that the duo was given a lot of freedom to explore.

The score album

The score gets its own release, separate from The Album, and it comes out later: on 11 December 2026, not at launch.

  • Tracks: 30, plus a number of digital bonus tracks that Rockstar says are included across all formats.
  • Formats: streaming, a 2-CD set, a triple-black 3-LP vinyl (sold on the Rockstar Store) and a limited "crystal clear" 3-LP, according to Rockstar and VGC. Pre-orders opened in late October.
  • Singles: "Flower in a Hurricane" (15 October) and "Pusher" featuring Gesaffelstein (29 October).

GTA VI: The Festival in Miami

To celebrate launch, Rockstar is putting on a real music festival in Miami: Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, presented with PlayStation and produced by the team behind Miami's III Points festival. Rockstar announced it on 9 November, and Stereogum and others covered it the next day.

  • Date: Saturday 21 November 2026, two days after the game comes out.
  • Venue: Mana Wynwood, 318 NW 23rd St, Miami, FL 33127. Entry is via 5th Avenue.
  • Stages: two stages of nonstop music. The stages haven't been named.
  • Doors: 4 pm ET, according to the DICE listing. Set times and stage assignments haven't been published.

Tickets

If you were hoping to just buy a ticket, that window has gone. Here's how it worked:

  • Entry was through a pre-sale lottery at gtavi-thefestival.miami, with codes sent out by Laylo. Entries closed on Tuesday 10 November at 4 pm ET, and winners were drawn at 7 pm ET.
  • Winners could buy tickets on DICE from Wednesday 11 November at 12 pm ET, with a DICE account using the same email and phone number as their lottery entry.
  • The limit was two tickets per person, and tickets are non-transferable.
  • Lottery entries are closed, and Rockstar's page now points to a waitlist on DICE.

Rockstar doesn't list a price. The DICE listing shows $72 a ticket, all in, while GGRecon reported $60 before sales opened, so treat any figure as a guide rather than official.

Rules if you're going

  • Entry is 17+, and 21+ for alcohol. Bring a valid government photo ID; digital IDs aren't accepted.
  • Clear bags only, up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches.
  • No re-entry, no refunds, and no moshing or crowd-surfing.
  • There's no parking on site.
  • Accessibility wristbands are handed out at the Accessibility Services Hub.
  • The latest policies are on the III Points festival page (iiipoints.com/gtavi-thefestival).

Is it being streamed?

No stream has been announced. Neither Rockstar's page nor the DICE listing mentions one, according to GGRecon. Don't mix it up with YouTube's separate 24-hour GTA 6 launch livestream, which starts with a pre-show hosted by RDCWorld at 11 pm ET on 18 November, according to RockstarINTEL and Vice. That's about the game's launch, not the festival.

The Daft Punk story

In October a story spread that Daft Punk had been Rockstar's first choice for the score, had been offered an unlimited budget, and had said no, leaving Justice as second choice. It's a good story, but it doesn't stand up.

  • Where it came from: a single post on X by a music-rumour account on 19 October 2026, which was then widely shared.
  • What others said: EDM.com said the claim remains unsubstantiated, Gameranx ran it as a rumour, and GTABoom called it unverified.
  • Official word: there's been no statement from Rockstar, Daft Punk's representatives or Justice, according to GTABoom.

There is a real Daft Punk link, though. Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo, one half of the duo, produced Travis Scott's "RHYNO", one of the first singles from The Album, according to Rockstar and Music Business Worldwide. So a former Daft Punk member is part of GTA 6's music. Daft Punk as a duo isn't, and there's no evidence they were ever offered the score.
