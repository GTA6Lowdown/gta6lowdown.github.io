GTA 6: everything we know

Updated 16 November 2026. Everything here is as of 16 November 2026, three days before launch.

Grand Theft Auto VI comes out on Thursday 19 November 2026, at midnight local time, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It's a single-player game about Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, set in Vice City and the wider state of Leonida. There's no PC version announced, and Rockstar hasn't said anything about an online mode.

Quick answers

  • Release: 19 November 2026, midnight local time, after two delays (from 2025, then from 26 May 2026).
  • Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming. No PS4 version.
  • Performance: Rockstar says it's targeting 30 fps, and it hasn't announced a faster mode for any console. The PS5 Pro gets sharper image quality and ray-traced shadows.
  • PC: not announced. A Q1 2027 window is only a report from GamesBeat, based on anonymous sources.
  • Price: $79.99 for the Standard Edition, $99.99 for the digital-only Ultimate Edition.
  • Pre-load: open since 10 November. Rockstar says it needs up to 212 GB of free space, depending on your device.
  • Online: Rockstar calls GTA VI a single-player experience and has announced nothing about an online mode.
  • Music: 34-track album out on launch day; Justice's score album follows on 11 December 2026.

Release date history

GTA 6 has had a long road. Rockstar first said a new Grand Theft Auto was "well underway" in February 2022, then revealed it with Trailer 1 in December 2023, aiming for 2025. It slipped twice before landing on 19 November 2026.

DateWhat happened

4 Dec 2023Trailer 1, released a little earlier than planned (it was due on 5 December). Release window: 2025, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
2 May 2025First delay, from autumn 2025 to 26 May 2026. Rockstar apologises and says it needs the time for quality.
6 Nov 2025Second delay, to Thursday 19 November 2026, for extra polish.
18 Jun 2026Cover art revealed.

19 Nov 2026Launch, midnight local time.

Both delays came with the same reason: more time to reach the quality players expect. With development finished and pre-load live, there's no sign of a third one.

What time does it unlock?

Rockstar Support says midnight local time on 19 November, in all regions, on both consoles. One wrinkle: according to Forbes, US storefronts treat the whole country as one Eastern-time region, so players on the US West Coast may be able to start at 9 p.m. PT on 18 November. Rockstar hasn't addressed that, so check the countdown on your console. Xbox Cloud Gaming starts at 1 a.m. ET on 19 November. Our GTA 6 launch day guide has a full time-zone table.

Platforms and performance

GTA VI is on two console families only: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. PlayStation's own page says it's PS5-only on PlayStation, so there's no PS4 version, and there's no native Xbox One version either.

  • Frame rate: Rockstar says it's targeting 30 frames per second on both platforms, while pushing them to their limits. It gives no separate frame-rate target for the PS5 Pro, and outlets such as VGC report 30 fps on every console. Rockstar hasn't announced a 40 or 60 fps mode, and it hasn't given a rendering resolution.
  • PS5 Pro: PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) for a cleaner image, plus ray-traced shadows that only the Pro gets. It's a picture upgrade, not a frame-rate one.
  • DualSense: full haptics (adjustable in the accessibility settings), adaptive triggers, the controller speaker, Tempest 3D Audio and faster loading.
  • Xbox Cloud Gaming: you can stream it on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One, using Series S graphics settings. Rockstar warns that your connection speed can affect how it runs.
  • Remote Play: supported on PlayStation and Xbox.
  • Captured on PS5: Rockstar says Trailer 2 and the Extended Look were captured in-game on PS5. According to Vice, Rockstar said the Launch Trailer came from a standard PS5, not a Pro.

Storage and pre-load

Pre-load opened on 10 November: at midnight local time on PS5, and at 12 a.m. GMT worldwide on Xbox (7 p.m. ET on 9 November in the US). Rockstar's figure is that the pre-load needs up to 212 GB of available space, depending on your device. That's an upper limit for the pre-load, not a final install size, and Rockstar hasn't given separate sizes for PS5, Series X or Series S. Pre-loading doesn't let you play early; the game stays locked until launch.

Physical copies and rating

The boxed version has no disc. It's a download code in a box. PlayStation codes are region-locked; Xbox codes aren't. The game is rated ESRB Mature, with in-game purchases noted on the rating.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not at launch, and Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version at all. Take-Two's results on 9 November 2026 list GTA VI for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. On the earnings call, Zelnick said the game is on two consoles when it launches, and that news about new versions will come from Rockstar itself.

According to GamesBeat (13 October 2026), four anonymous industry sources say a PC version is planned for the first quarter of 2027. Rockstar didn't comment. Treat that as a report, not a date. Zelnick said in May 2026 that Rockstar always starts on console to serve its core audience first, but that isn't an announcement either way.

An older claim, from Corsair's boss in 2025, said PC would arrive in early 2026. That never happened; the console version itself moved to November 2026.

What the Ultimate Edition adds

Rockstar says the extras are spread through Jason and Lucia's story, with new items unlocking chapter by chapter. Its support page lists:

  • '95 Grotti Cheetah, a Classic Car Collection and the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy with its own garage
  • Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers and Personalized Weapon Variants
  • Vice City Styles, Goodtime Gear and the Stock 305 Clothing Store
  • Jason's Safehouse Vehicles, the Ganado Retro Build and the Shitzu Squalo
  • Two mod shops (Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's), Sara's Unisex Salon, the Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor and the PTT Youngin$ Compound

According to VGC, the Electric Fang parlour has more than 50 tattoos by the artists FAILE, and One-Eyed Willie's sits in Lake Leonida. If you buy the Standard Edition, you can add the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later from the PlayStation or Microsoft store, even with a physical copy once the code is redeemed.

Pre-order and early bonuses

  • Vintage Vice City Pack: a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan with a garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. It's for every pre-order and purchase before 20 November 2026.
  • One month of GTA+: for digital pre-orders and purchases before 20 November only. On PlayStation you must claim it by 31 March 2027, it auto-renews, and it isn't offered in every country. It's for the existing GTA Online, not a GTA VI mode.
  • Free map: on launch day Rockstar adds a printable cover-art poster and map of Leonida as a free download on its site. A double-sided printed version follows from the Rockstar Store shortly after launch, for $0.99 or free with any store order.

The Vice City Collection box

Rockstar also sells a limited collector's box, "The Goodtime State: Vice City Collection", themed on the in-game TV show Macca the Gator. It holds 11 items, including a 6-inch Macca figure, Oakley Frogskins sunglasses, a New Era cap and a souvenir poster of Leonida. The game isn't included, so it isn't a collector's edition. According to VGC, it costs $399.99 in the US and £349.99 in the UK.

Story and characters

Rockstar's official premise is short. Jason and Lucia have always known the odds are against them. When an easy score goes wrong, they land on what Rockstar calls "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America", caught in a criminal conspiracy that stretches across Leonida. To get through it, they have to rely on each other more than ever.

Rupert Humphries, Rockstar's senior vice president of narrative, told Game Informer that the story is about rich and poor people living right next to each other, with crime and corruption linking them.

The two leads

  • Jason Duval grew up around grifters and crooks. After time in the Army, he ended up in the Keys working for local drug runners. He wants an easy life, but it keeps getting harder, and meeting Lucia could be the best or worst thing that ever happens to him. In the Launch Trailer he says he left the Army on bad terms and can't get a loan or steady work.
  • Lucia Caminos learned to fight from her father. Fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary, and luck got her out. She wants the good life her mother has dreamed of since their days in Liberty City, and sees Jason as a possible way there.

Setting: Leonida and Vice City

GTA 6 takes place in the state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida, nicknamed "the Goodtime State" in official material. Its big city is Vice City, and Rockstar has called the game a return to a modern-day Vice City (the series first went there in 2002).

Rockstar's site names six regions:

  • Vice City, the city itself
  • Leonida Keys, the island chain to the south
  • Grassrivers
  • Port Gellhorn
  • Ambrosia
  • Mount Kalaga, a national park

Other official place names include Watson Bay and Lake Leonida, both mentioned in the Ultimate Edition details.

How big is the map?

Aaron Garbut, co-head of Rockstar North, told Game Informer that Leonida is Rockstar's biggest world yet, about twice the size of GTA V's map, with the focus on density rather than square miles. Rob Nelson told IGN it's "a very big map". Rockstar hasn't given an area figure. Bigger comparisons you see online (three times Red Dead Redemption 2, for example) come second-hand, such as a YouTuber relaying a chat with Nelson, not from an official statement.

Gameplay systems Rockstar has described

Most of what we know about how GTA 6 plays comes from Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North, in interviews with IGN and the Japanese magazine Famitsu (in translation), and from Rockstar North leads in Game Informer's cover story. Here's the short version.

Switching between Jason and Lucia

Nelson told Famitsu that switching can be restricted depending on where you are in the story, and some missions switch for you. He also described a car chase where one drives while the other shoots, with control passing from Jason to Lucia.

Police, Heat and Wanted

  • Nelson calls the police search state "Heat". It stays on while you're inside the Heat area.
  • A crime only makes you wanted if someone sees it or a security system picks it up, much like Red Dead Redemption 2.
  • Police remember what you were wearing. A new haircut won't fool them, but a fresh outfit can clear your Heat. Splitting Jason and Lucia up, or ditching the car they saw, also helps.
  • Previews (Vice and Game Informer coverage among them) report a maximum of six wanted stars. Rockstar hasn't stated the number itself.

The Criminal Profile

A new system tracks what kind of criminal you are, for example professional or violent, and shapes how people and the world react. Nelson says it isn't a good-versus-evil meter, and that it shares some ideas with Red Dead Redemption 2's Honor system without being the same. According to Vice's roundup of previews, violence a mission requires doesn't count against you; needless violence does.

Weapons

Nelson told Famitsu (in translation) that, for the first time in the series, you can't keep your whole arsenal hidden on you. Long guns go in Ammu-Nation lockers or the boot of your personal vehicle, and walking around with a gun out makes people steer clear or react. Exact carry limits you may have read come from previews; Rockstar hasn't published a number.

Robberies, phones and the world

  • Store robberies: blow the safe (faster and louder, and you may get less) or find the code or lean on staff (slower, pays more). Take too long and police may surround the store.
  • Snapmatic: it's back. You can browse and follow accounts, but as a couple on the run you can't post; Nelson says you just lurk.
  • Weather: local, persistent weather, with tropical storms, hurricanes and simulated wind (Owen Shepherd, Rockstar's VP of Art for lighting and rendering, to Game Informer).
  • Wildlife: more than 170 animal species, with rare, discoverable and legendary animals to track, according to Game Informer.
  • Interiors: Garbut says there's a critical mass of interiors and everything is "dialed up".
  • Activities: according to Extended Look coverage, things like scuba diving, jet skis, airboats, kayaking, mini golf, gyms, base jumping and zoos. Game Informer coverage also mentions fast travel through a ride-share app.

Microtransactions and AI

Zelnick has said generative AI has no part in what Rockstar is building. On microtransactions, Kinda Funny's hosts relayed that Nelson said there's no monetisation in single player at launch, as reported by IGN and NME. Rockstar declined to talk about online. The rating still lists in-game purchases, which most likely covers the paid Ultimate Edition Upgrade.

Music and radio

Rockstar calls this its biggest and widest-ranging in-game radio yet. For the first time there are on-demand podcasts, and you can listen on foot as well as in cars, through headphones and earbuds linked to Jason's and Lucia's phones. Six stations have been named so far: Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics.

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, 34 original tracks made with Atlantic Records, is out on 19 November. Justice wrote the game's adaptive score, which reacts to how you play, and the 30-track score album is out on 11 December 2026. To celebrate, Rockstar and PlayStation are holding GTA VI: The Festival at Mana Wynwood in Miami on 21 November.

Hosts, formats and the festival line-up are all in our GTA 6 music and radio guide.

Online

Rockstar describes GTA VI as a single-player experience, and PlayStation's FAQ says the same. Rockstar has announced nothing about an online mode for GTA VI: no name, no date, no details.

The existing GTA Online carries on. Zelnick said on 9 November that Rockstar will keep supporting it with new content and events; its last big update was the Kortz Center Heist on 14 July 2026.

There are reports about a GTA VI online mode. According to Digital Trends, a Bloomberg Originals documentary says multiplayer comes "a year later", and Twitch's CEO has reportedly pointed to 2027. None of this comes from Rockstar or Take-Two. Zelnick's line on what Rockstar does next is that it will be announced "in the fullness of time".

What hasn't been announced

Three days out, a few big questions are still open. Rockstar hasn't announced:

  • a PC version or date;
  • any online mode for GTA VI;
  • a 60 fps or 40 fps mode on any console;
  • a rendering resolution, or a final install size per console;
  • an official map size in square miles or kilometres;
  • the full radio station list beyond the six named;
  • cheat codes;
  • sales or pre-order numbers. Take-Two only says pre-orders are far beyond its expectations, and figures you see online are analytics estimates.
