GTA 6 vs GTA 5: The Biggest Differences

GUIDEBy GTA 6 Lowdown · Written 22 Nov 2026

The short answer

GTA 6 swaps GTA 5's three leads for two, Jason and Lucia, and moves from Los Santos to Vice City and the state of Leonida, a map that Rockstar's Rob Nelson reportedly told the YouTuber TGG (The Gaming Gorilla) is twice the size of GTA 5's. The bigger change is how it plays: police only react to crimes that a witness saw or an alarm reported, you can't carry your whole arsenal, some parked cars need tools to steal, and there's no mission replay and, reportedly, no full first-person mode.

  • Map: reportedly twice GTA 5's (Rob Nelson, via TGG); VGC says Vice City alone is twice the size of Los Santos.
  • Leads: two, who can play together or apart, instead of three separate lives.
  • Police: a witness system, and icons showing what they know about you.
  • Missing: mission replay, medals, a full first-person mode (reportedly) and, so far, online.

How we did this: GTA 6 facts come from Rockstar's own statements and from reviews and launch guides, each named below. Anything only reported by an outlet is labelled that way.

Is the GTA 6 map bigger than GTA 5?

Yes, about twice the size. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson shared size comparisons with TGG ahead of the Extended Look, as VGC reported: the world is twice GTA 5's and three times Red Dead Redemption 2's. VGC also reports, without saying who gave the figures, that Vice City alone is twice the size of Los Santos and that, with its suburbs, it is eleven times larger than Los Santos and its surrounding areas (a comparison of built-up areas, not of the whole map).

We haven't found a size in square kilometres from Rockstar. Any exact number you see is an outside estimate.

Two leads instead of three

Nelson told IGN that GTA 5's three characters were aligned but lived very separate lives, while GTA 6 is about a couple. There's a ton to do as Jason and Lucia on their own or together, he says, and they have a relationship system that you build by spending time together. IGN's Rachel Weber notes the romance is entirely optional. You can switch between them too: in IGN's demo, Nelson switched to the passenger seat and Jason drove.

How are the police different?

GTA 6 takes its approach from Red Dead Redemption 2, Nelson told Famitsu. A crime needs a witness or an alarm before it's reported to the police, he told IGN. The police then work from what they know, shown as icons under your stars, such as what you're driving and whether you're a couple. Buying and wearing a new outfit removes your Heat.

It means changing what the police know can matter as much as outrunning them. Our police and Heat guide goes through it all.

Can you carry all your guns, like in GTA 5?

Not all of them. Nelson told IGN that all the weapons you've bought are always available in the back of your personal vehicle, and Famitsu mentions Ammu-Nation lockers too. Nelson told TGG that GTA 5's approach doesn't work at this resolution, GamesRadar reported. Exactly how many you can carry isn't settled: TGG reported two concealed handguns and two long guns (one on the back, one in hand), while Famitsu says long guns can't be hidden and are stored in an Ammu-Nation locker or the trunk.

Is stealing cars different?

Yes. In earlier GTAs you could press one button to hotwire a car automatically. Nelson told IGN that left a lot of potential gameplay on the table, so in GTA 6 some parked cars need tools you get through the story, such as a key cloner or a slim jim, though you can always carjack a driver. A car with a tracker leads the police to you if you drive slowly, and Nelson says the streets are narrower and busier than before.

Does money matter more?

Yes. Nelson told IGN that money matters more in this story than in previous GTA games, and that parts of the game need you to make money to move the story on. VGC's launch guide says you lose the cash you're carrying if you die or get arrested, and that you're cash only until an early mission unlocks bank accounts. There's also the Criminal Profile, which tracks what sort of criminal you are. Famitsu reports it is the first system in the series to track part of how you play.

What does GTA 5 have that GTA 6 doesn't?

  • Mission replay and medals. Gone. Save manually before missions you might want to see again.
  • Full first-person mode. GTA 5 added it in 2014. VICE reports that Rob Nelson said the game is built around third-person play, with no full first-person mode at launch, only first-person views when aiming some weapons.
  • Online. GTA Online came two weeks after GTA 5. Nothing has been announced for GTA 6.
  • PC. GTA 5 is on PC. GTA 6 isn't, yet.

The full list is in our missing features guide.

Is GTA 6 better than GTA 5?

Critics think so. GTA 6 launched with 99 on Metacritic, against GTA 5's 97, and IGN and GameSpot both gave it 10/10. The complaints are worth knowing if you loved GTA 5's pace: reviewers named clunky combat (The Verge), stiff bike handling (IGN), strict missions (Game Informer) and, at Worthplaying, a slow start. Our review round-up has the details.
