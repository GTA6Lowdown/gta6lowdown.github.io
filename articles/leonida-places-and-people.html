Places and people guide

LEONIDA: THE PLACES AND PEOPLE OF GTA 6

Updated as of 16 November 2026, three days before launch on 19 November.

Images: Rockstar Games

GTA 6 is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional take on a sunshine state, with Vice City as its biggest city. Rockstar has named six regions and profiled eight characters, starting with the two leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Here's what Rockstar itself says about each place and person, which real Florida spots people compare them to, and who else has turned up in the official videos.

Quick answers

  • Where is GTA 6 set? The state of Leonida. Its main city is Vice City.
  • Is Leonida Florida? Rockstar never says "Florida", but press and fans everywhere read it as Rockstar's version of it.
  • How many regions? Six: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.
  • How big is the map? Rockstar's Aaron Garbut told Game Informer it's about twice the size of GTA V's. There's no official square-mile figure.
  • How many characters has Rockstar profiled? Eight: Jason, Lucia, Cal, Boobie, Dre'Quan, Real Dimez, Raul and Brian.
  • Who are the voice actors? Rockstar hasn't confirmed any of the cast.
  • Can you visit Liberty City? Rockstar hasn't said. It's only mentioned in Lucia's backstory.

The state of Leonida

Leonida is the fictional US state GTA 6 takes place in. Rockstar's own site sells it like a tourist board would, inviting you to see the sunshine state's must-see destinations, and it pitches Vice City as the dark side of the sunniest place in America.

The story starts there too. Going by Trailer 2's official description, Jason and Lucia have always known the odds were stacked against them. When an easy job goes wrong, they end up caught in a criminal conspiracy that runs right across Leonida, and the only way out is to rely on each other.

A few other official names fill in the world around the six regions:

  • Leonida Penitentiary, the prison where Lucia served time. It's named in her profile.
  • Liberty City, which exists in this universe. Lucia's profile mentions her family's past there, but Rockstar hasn't said whether you can go.

Fan map guides also list county names they've spotted in trailer footage, such as Vice-Dale, Leonard and Mariana. Rockstar hasn't explained those, so treat them as unconfirmed until you see them in the game.

How big is the map?

Here's the one figure that's on record. In Game Informer's preview (issue 382, September 2026), Rockstar North's co-studio head Aaron Garbut said Leonida is the studio's biggest world, about twice the size of GTA V's map. He also said the team cared more about density, how much there is to do and see, than about raw square miles. Forbes and GTAVice.net both reported the same comments.

That's as far as it goes. Rockstar hasn't given an area in square miles or kilometres, and it hadn't shown a full world map as of 16 November 2026. (Rockstar has said a free digital map of Leonida comes on launch day, so that should settle it.) You'll see bigger, more exact numbers online. They come from fan estimates and we don't repeat them here.

The six regions

Rockstar revealed six regions on 6 May 2025, alongside Trailer 2. The region pages on the official site have since been trimmed down to buttons, so the descriptions below come from the text as Game Informer and MP1st reported it that day. The two agree on the details.

One thing to keep in mind as you read: the Florida comparisons are not Rockstar's. Rockstar never names a real place. They come from press and fan sites, and on some regions those sites don't agree.

Vice City

Images: Rockstar Games

Rockstar sums Vice City up as American glamour, hustle and greed squeezed into one place. It named four areas:

  • Ocean Beach: pastel art deco hotels and white sand.
  • Little Cuba: busy streets full of bakeries.
  • The Tisha-Wocka flea market: the place for knock-off brands.
  • VC Port: billed as the cruise ship capital of the world.

The real-world comparison: nobody doubts Vice City is Miami. According to GTA Intel, Forbes and fan wikis, Ocean Beach lines up with South Beach and the Ocean Drive art deco strip, and Little Cuba with Little Havana. Fan wikis also match Tisha-Wocka with the Opa-locka flea market, and fans read VC Port as PortMiami. Rockstar lists Little Cuba and Tisha-Wocka as separate places, so we do too.

Leonida Keys

Images: Rockstar Games

Rockstar describes the Keys as a relaxed chain of tropical islands where life is easy-going rather than showy. The catch is that they sit next to some of the most beautiful, and most dangerous, waters in America. Game Informer picked out retirees, casual boating and sea turtles from Rockstar's text and screenshots.

This is Jason's home turf. His profile says he works for drug runners in the Keys, and Brian's profile ties his smuggling to the Keys too.

The real-world comparison: the Florida Keys. Game Informer called it a parody of them, and GTA Intel compares some of the shoreline to Key Largo.

Grassrivers

Grassrivers is Rockstar's wild, ancient-feeling swamp. Alligators are its best-known residents, but Rockstar hints that the mangroves hide predators that are more dangerous. Rockstar's launch gallery on 5 November 2026 appears to include shots from Grassrivers too, though the pictures have no captions.

The real-world comparison: the Everglades. The name itself seems to nod to the Everglades' old nickname, the "River of Grass".

Port Gellhorn

Port Gellhorn is a holiday hotspot that's long past its best: cheap motels, shut-down attractions and empty strip malls. Rockstar jokes that the local economy now runs on malt liquor, painkillers and truck-stop energy drinks. Fan guides record Rockstar calling it Leonida's forgotten coast.

The real-world comparison: this is where the comparisons split. It's most often linked to the Florida Panhandle on the Gulf coast. GTA Intel points to Apalachicola and Franklin County, which really are nicknamed the Forgotten Coast, while other guides suggest Panama City. Call it Panhandle-ish and leave it there.

Ambrosia

Images: Rockstar Games

Ambrosia sits in the heart of Leonida, a town where old American industry and old-school values still call the shots. The Allied Crystal sugar refinery provides most of the jobs. A local biker gang, in Rockstar's telling, provides most of everything else. Fan wikis such as GTABase name the gang the Final Chapter MC, from the patches in the screenshots. Rockstar's own text doesn't name it.

The real-world comparison: the sugar country around Lake Okeechobee, especially Clewiston, according to GTA Intel and several map guides.

Mount Kalaga National Park

Rockstar places Mount Kalaga on Leonida's northern border. It's a national landmark with hunting, fishing and off-road trails. In the backwoods you'll find hillbilly mystics and paranoid radicals keeping their distance from the government.

The real-world comparison: this one's an open question, because Florida doesn't have mountains. Guides usually point at North Florida and the Georgia border. GTA Intel admits it's guesswork.

Named spots from the later videos

Rockstar hasn't added a seventh region since May 2025. The later videos have named some new spots inside the existing ones, though:

  • Bayside: a Vice City neighbourhood Lucia names during a police chase in the Extended Look (27 August 2026), with shops and a beach nearby, according to SVG.
  • Effluvia: a rooftop bar where Jason and Lucia meet Brian in the Extended Look, according to SVG.
  • Megamundo's first US headquarters: a TV network's Vice City offices, where the Extended Look's opening-party sequence happens.
  • Brian's Marina: the boatyard sign seen in the opening seconds of the Launch Trailer (15 October 2026).
  • Watson Bay and Lake Leonida: named in Rockstar's Ultimate Edition text as the homes of a garage and a mod shop.

The Launch Gallery of 5 November 2026 also shows Vice City, Grassrivers, the Keys and Port Gellhorn again, but none of the pictures are captioned.

The eight profiled characters

Rockstar's people page has eight profiles, and as of 16 November 2026 none have been added since May 2025. The Extended Look didn't come with any new bios either. Every summary below is our paraphrase of Rockstar's own profile text. Rockstar also hasn't confirmed who voices or plays any of them, so any actor names you've seen are guesses.

Jason Duval

Lead character. Jason wants an easy life, but things keep getting harder. He grew up around grifters and crooks. The Army was supposed to straighten out his troubled teens and didn't. Now he's in the Keys working for local drug runners, and he might be ready for something different. Rockstar says meeting Lucia could be the best or the worst thing that's ever happened to him.

Links: co-lead with Lucia. Lives rent-free at one of Brian's places in return for help with shakedowns. Friends with Cal.

Lucia Caminos

Images: Rockstar Games

Lead character. Lucia's father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk. Fighting for her family landed her in Leonida Penitentiary, and, in Rockstar's words, luck got her out. Not parole. Now she wants the good life her mother has dreamed of since their Liberty City days, and she plans to get it with careful, calculated moves. A life with Jason could be her way out.

Links: co-lead with Jason.

Cal Hampton

Jason's friend and one of Brian's associates. Cal's happiest at home with a beer and his private browser tabs, listening in on Coast Guard radio. He's a conspiracy-minded homebody who's content at the bottom of America, while Jason has bigger plans.

Links: Jason's friend; works with Brian.

Boobie Ike

A Vice City legend who plays the part. Boobie turned his street past into a legitimate empire: property, a strip club and a recording studio. He's friendly right up until it's time to talk business. The thing he's most invested in is Only Raw Records, his label with Dre'Quan, and it needs a hit.

Links: Dre'Quan's business partner. In the Extended Look, Jason and Lucia take on a drug pickup for him, according to Wikipedia's summary of the video.

Dre'Quan Priest

More hustler than gangster. Dre'Quan dealt to get by, but music was always the goal. He's signed the Real Dimez to Only Raw Records and wants to make his name in Vice City's music scene. If it works, he can stop booking acts into Boobie's club.

Links: Boobie's partner and the Real Dimez's label boss. His profile doesn't tie him to Jason or Lucia directly.

Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy)

Two friends since high school who turned scamming local dealers into a career, thanks to provocative rap and a relentless social media presence. An early hit with local rapper DWNPLY made their name, and five years of trouble followed. Now they're signed to Only Raw Records and chasing a second hit.

Links: signed to Dre'Quan and Boobie's label. No stated link to the leads.

Raul Bautista

A confident, charming and very experienced bank robber who's always looking for people willing to take big risks for big rewards. His reckless style raises the stakes on every job, and Rockstar warns that his crew will soon have to go all in or get out.

Links: in the Extended Look, Jason and Lucia drive a getaway for him, according to Wikipedia's summary of the video.

Brian Heder

An old-school drug runner from the Keys' smuggling heyday. Brian still moves product through his boat yard with his third wife, Lori, and these days he lets other people do the dirty work. He lets Jason live rent-free at one of his properties as long as Jason helps with local shakedowns and drops by for Lori's sangria.

Links: Jason's landlord, with Jason helping on his shakedowns; Cal is also one of his associates.

Other characters in the official videos

A few more names come up in the Extended Look and the trailers. Rockstar hasn't published profiles for any of them, so the details below are how outlets have described what they saw and heard. They could change once the game is out.

  • Lori Heder: Brian's wife. Her name is official, since it's in Brian's profile. Gfinity lists her among the Extended Look's new faces, and SVG notes a woman with Brian at Effluvia who may be his wife.
  • Andrés de León: described by Screen Rant and SVG as a Megamundo executive, and by Screen Rant as egotistical. Jason and Lucia are hired as his private security, and the party at Megamundo's headquarters goes badly wrong.
  • Valentina: the woman who hires Jason and Lucia to guard Andrés, according to Screen Rant. Screen Rant and Gfinity both say she's a returning character from GTA Online.
  • Mendez: a Vice City police figure, seen in Trailer 2 and named in the Extended Look, where Mendez is involved in a raid, according to Screen Rant.

Brands, businesses and apps

A GTA world is half parody brands. Here's what's been named so far, starting with the ones Rockstar has written down itself.

Named by Rockstar

  • Only Raw Records: Boobie and Dre'Quan's label, from the official profiles.
  • Allied Crystal: the sugar refinery behind Ambrosia's jobs, from the region text.
  • Rockstar Store edition items: the Ultimate Edition list names Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's (mod shops), Sara's Unisex Salon, Stock 305 (a clothing store), Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor, Goodtime Gear, the PTT Youngin$ Compound, and vehicle makers Vapid, Grotti and Shitzu. The store's list gives names only; Rockstar's longer Ultimate Edition text describes a few of them.

Seen in the official videos

  • Megamundo: a Spanish-language TV network opening its first US headquarters in Vice City. Fan wikis compare it to the real network Telemundo.
  • Effluvia (the rooftop bar) and Brian's Marina.
  • Social media clips: Trailer 1 framed much of its footage as in-world social media videos, recreating real Florida viral moments, as PC Gamer pointed out at the time.
  • Returning parody brands: according to fan breakdowns, familiar names such as eCola, Sprunk, Pißwasser, Weazel News and iFruit turn up in the footage.

Reported apps

Fan breakdowns of the Extended Look and press previews have reported several phone apps: Snapmatic (photos), Ryde Me (ride-hailing) and BuckMe (money). Some lists say BuckMe came from a creator preview rather than the Extended Look itself. Rockstar hasn't explained most of these, so we'll only know exactly what they do at launch.

Vice City, in real Miami

Not in the game, but fun: in the run-up to launch, Rockstar has been dressing real Miami up as Vice City. VGC reported a Miami Heat "A Night In Vice City" game on launch eve. Visitors have also spotted Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell inside the Kaseya Center and in-game brands on billboards around the city, but VGC treats those as sightings, not Rockstar announcements.

Popular fan theories

These are theories, not facts. Rockstar hasn't backed any of them, and we'll find out on 19 November.

  • Raul will double-cross Jason and Lucia. A favourite, built on comparing trailer shots. Some fans flip it the other way round.
  • Jason is the traitor, or an undercover cop. Plenty of discussion, no evidence from Rockstar.
  • Cal is a secret third playable character. Rockstar has only ever presented two leads.
  • Multiple or tragic endings. Pure speculation for now.
  • The Real Dimez are based on a real Miami rap duo. Music and fan sites compare them to City Girls. Rockstar hasn't said.
  • Liberty City will be visitable. Its only official mention is in Lucia's backstory.
  • A neighbouring state called Gloriana. GTA Intel says the name appears on licence plates, but it isn't a confirmed region.
