Every GTA Game in Story Order

GUIDEBy GTA 6 Lowdown · Written 22 Nov 2026 · No story spoilers

The short answer

There isn't one GTA timeline. There are three, which fans call the 2D, 3D and HD universes. Each has its own versions of the cities and its own stories, though some names, brands and background characters turn up in more than one. Guides such as GTABase put GTA 6 in the HD universe, with GTA IV and GTA V, and Rockstar describes it as a return to modern-day Vice City.

  • 2D universe: the top-down games, released 1997 to 1999.
  • 3D universe: GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas and their spin-offs, set from 1984 to 2001.
  • HD universe: GTA IV, GTA V, GTA Online and GTA 6.
  • Before GTA 6: you don't need to play any of the others first.

How we did this: setting years come from GamesRadar, GTABase and Wikipedia, plus fan wikis where we say so. Where a game never states its year, we say that too. "Universe" is the name fans and wikis use; Rockstar doesn't put it on the box.

Why are there three GTA timelines?

Because the world has been started over twice. GTA III, in 2001, introduced a new 3D Liberty City, and the PS2 and PSP games that followed shared that version of the world. GTA IV, in 2008, made a new Liberty City again, in much more detail. The first GTA already had Liberty City, San Andreas and Vice City, so each city exists in more than one universe.

GTA IV's two add-ons run alongside its main story in the same city, and were later sold together as Episodes from Liberty City. GTA IV never states its year on screen, but it's generally placed around 2008. Fans generally count Chinatown Wars as HD universe too, because it uses GTA IV's Liberty City. GTA Online is set around the time of GTA V and has kept getting updates since 2013.

Rockstar hasn't given GTA 6 a year. It calls it a return to modern-day Vice City.

The first GTA doesn't state a year, but GamesRadar places it around the time it came out. GTA 2's year isn't clear either: GamesRadar says the clues point to 1999 or 2013. The London games were add-ons: London 1969 for the original GTA, and London 1961 for London 1969, Wikipedia says.

How many times has GTA been to Vice City?

Four times, counting GTA 6. The first GTA in 1997 had a top-down Vice City. Then came Vice City (2002) and Vice City Stories (2006) in the 3D universe, both set in the 1980s. GTA 6's Vice City is the first in the HD universe. Stations such as Flash FM are back on the radio, but it's a different world. Our music and radio guide has the returning stations.

Which order should I play them in?

For most people, release order is the best way in, because you see the series grow up. If you want story order, play each universe on its own: the 3D games from Vice City Stories to GTA III, then the HD games from GTA IV to GTA V. You don't need any of them to follow GTA 6.

STILL PLAYABLE: GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas are in Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition, and GTA V and GTA Online are on current consoles and PC, Wikipedia says. Availability of the older handheld games varies, so check your store.
