Is GTA 6 on PC?

GUIDEBy GTA 6 Lowdown · Written 22 Nov 2026

The short answer

No, not yet. GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version, a date or PC specs. GamesBeat has reported a PC launch in the first three months of 2027, from anonymous sources, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed it. Xbox One owners can stream the game through Xbox Cloud Gaming, but that doesn't work on PC.

  • Official platforms: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
  • PC: not announced. Take-Two says platform news comes from Rockstar.
  • Reported window: Q1 2027, according to GamesBeat's four unnamed industry sources.
  • Xbox One: playable through Xbox Cloud Gaming, on Xbox consoles only.

How we did this: we've used Rockstar's and Take-Two's own statements and the Xbox Cloud Gaming announcement, and labelled reports as reports.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

No. Rockstar's launch press release names PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar hasn't put out a PC price, store listing or system requirements.

Take-Two hasn't ruled a PC version out. CEO Strauss Zelnick told Variety that information always comes from Take-Two's labels, TweakTown reports, meaning Rockstar. In a Bloomberg interview in May, he said Rockstar has historically released games on console first, and that the console-first launch wasn't related to Take-Two's marketing deal with Sony.

When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Officially, there's no date. The one specific report is from GamesBeat, which first published it on 13 October and said Rockstar is targeting the first quarter of 2027 for PC, citing four industry sources who asked not to be named. IBTimes UK repeated it on 20 November. If that's right, it would be a much faster turnaround than Rockstar's last two big games:

GameConsole launchPC launch
GTA 5September 2013April 2015
Red Dead Redemption 2October 2018November 2019
GTA 6November 2026Not announced (reported: Q1 2027)

Zelnick reportedly said PC can now make up 45 to 50% of sales for a big Take-Two game, according to GuruFocus's account of a Bloomberg interview. That's a reason a PC version matters to Take-Two, not a date.

Can I play GTA 6 on Xbox One or through the cloud?

On Xbox One, yes. Rockstar confirmed on 10 November that GTA 6 streams through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles, using Xbox Series S graphics settings. Outlets including KeenGamer and Dexerto note you still need to buy the game, and Dexerto adds that you need Game Pass or paid streaming time too.

Unusually, the cloud version is only for Xbox consoles, not PCs, phones or TVs. Windows Central says that may make it the only Xbox Cloud Gaming title with that restriction.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC with Remote Play?

Rockstar says GTA 6 fully supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox. That streams the game from your own console to another device. Windows Central and KeenGamer say that includes a PC, while Dexerto calls PC support unconfirmed. Either way, you still need the console and the game; the PC would just be a screen.

WATCH OUT

"GTA 6 PC download" sites and "PC ports". There's no official PC version, so anything offered as one isn't the real game, and could be a scam or malware. Don't download it, and don't pay for it.
