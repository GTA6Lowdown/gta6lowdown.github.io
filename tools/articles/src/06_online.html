The short answer

There's no date. GTA 6 launched on 19 November as a single-player game, and Rockstar hasn't announced an online mode, a date or a name for one. The only timing anyone has put on record, "next year", came from Twitch's CEO, not Rockstar. Meanwhile, the GTA Online you already know keeps running.

  • At launch: no online mode. Rockstar's pre-order announcement says GTA 6 features a single-player experience.
  • Release date: none from Rockstar or Take-Two.
  • "Next year": Twitch CEO Dan Clancy's expectation, reported by GTABase. Not an announcement.
  • GTA Online: still running on the consoles it's already on.

How we did this: we went through what Rockstar, Take-Two and their executives have said on the record, and kept that separate from reports. We don't use anything from the 2022 hack.

Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?

Not at launch. When Rockstar announced pre-orders in June, its press release said GTA 6 features a single-player experience, and that's what came out on 19 November. There's no online mode.

That isn't new for Rockstar. GTA 5 also launched as a single-player game, and GTA Online followed about two weeks later. The difference this time is that Rockstar hasn't announced anything about an online mode.

When is GTA 6 Online coming out?

There's no official date or window. Here's what has been said, and by whom:

  • Twitch CEO Dan Clancy told Bloomberg in September that he expects the next GTA multiplayer to arrive next year, after talking with Rockstar. GTABase, which reported it, is careful to say Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.
  • Jason Schreier, Bloomberg's games reporter, said Rockstar wasn't planning to talk about the new GTA Online any time soon, Outlook Respawn reported. He didn't say it had been delayed.

What happens to GTA Online now?

It keeps running. Take-Two's own reports list it on PS4, PS5, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, and it's on PC too. NoobFeed reports that on the November earnings call, Zelnick said Rockstar intends to keep supporting GTA Online.

"Your GTA Online money and cars carry over to GTA 6." Nothing like that has been announced. Rockstar hasn't said anything about moving characters, money, property or vehicles from GTA Online into a new online mode. Don't spend your GTA$ on the strength of a rumour.

Could GTA 6 Online come out with the PC version?

Possibly, but that's guesswork. GamesBeat has reported, from four anonymous industry sources, that the PC version is planned for the first three months of 2027, and IBTimes UK has repeated that report. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it. See our PC guide for details.
