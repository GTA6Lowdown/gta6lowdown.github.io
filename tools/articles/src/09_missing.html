The short answer

The confirmed ones: no mission replay menu and no mission medals, according to launch guides. Rockstar launched it as a single-player game on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and says the boxed version contains a download code. Reports also say there's no full first-person mode. You can't carry your whole arsenal like in GTA 5, and the game targets 30fps. Plenty of other "missing features" are going round, but we couldn't confirm most of them.

  • Rockstar's own pages: single-player only, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, a download code in the box.
  • Launch guides: no mission replay menu and no medals.
  • Reported: no full first-person mode.
  • Changed, not removed: weapons and saving.
  • Not confirmed: claims about prone, dual wielding and flipping cars back over.

How we did this: we only list something as missing if Rockstar said so, or a review or launch guide from a named outlet reported it. Anything that rests on a preview or an unnamed site is flagged.

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

Not from a menu. IGN's reviewer couldn't find a way to replay finished missions, and GTA Boom reports there's no mission replay menu at all, and no medals either.

Mission replay first arrived with GTA IV's The Ballad of Gay Tony in 2009, along with a scoring system, Engadget notes. GTA 5 had both replays and medals. We haven't found any explanation from Rockstar. Engadget and GTA Boom both suggest it may be because missions can play out differently depending on choices you made earlier, but that's their reading.

The workaround: make a manual save before any mission you might want to play again. You get 15 slots. See our beginner tips.

Does GTA 6 have first-person mode?

Not a full one, according to reports. VICE reported that Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told YouTuber Davy Jones there's no full first-person mode at launch, and that you still get a first-person view when aiming some weapons. That came through Davy Jones rather than an official Rockstar statement, as Notebookcheck pointed out at the time.

VGC's launch guide also describes a first-person look-around view you can use while standing still, which switches off as soon as you move.

Will a full mode come later? GTA 5's first-person mode arrived with the PS4 and Xbox One version in 2014. Rockstar hasn't said whether GTA 6 will get one.

Why can't I carry all my guns?

Because the bottomless pockets are gone. Nelson told IGN that all the weapons you've bought are always available in the back of your personal vehicle, and Famitsu's interview mentions Ammu-Nation lockers too. Nelson told TGG that GTA 5's approach doesn't work at this resolution, GamesRadar reported.

How much you can carry on you isn't settled. TGG reported two concealed handguns and two long guns, with one long gun on your back, while Famitsu's interview says weapons can no longer be hidden. Those two accounts don't match, and Rockstar hasn't given a clear answer.

Is there online multiplayer?

No. GTA 6 is a single-player game at launch, and Rockstar hasn't announced an online mode. Our GTA 6 Online guide has everything that has been said.

What else is different at launch?

  • No PC version. Rockstar's launch release names PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. See Is GTA 6 on PC?
  • A code in the box. Rockstar says the physical version contains a download code inside the box.
  • 30fps. VICE reported Nelson saying 30fps was the target on console, and TechRadar's review flags it for players who want smoother play.
  • No saving mid-mission. Manual saves are outside missions only, from the pause menu, and not at all during the prologue (KeenGamer).
