The short answer

If you're on PC, or you just haven't got GTA 6 yet, you can still go to Vice City tonight. GTA 6 is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, but the original Vice City from 2002 is the game that made the city famous, and it runs on almost anything. We're playing it from the very start, one or two missions per video, and this page follows along.

  • The game: GTA Vice City, first out on PS2 in October 2002, then PC in May 2003.
  • The setting: Vice City in 1986, with Tommy Vercetti fresh out of prison.
  • Our series: episode 1 covers the opening; episode 2 covers An Old Friend and The Party.
  • Playing it today: the version on sale now is the Definitive Edition, on PC and consoles.

How we did this: release dates come from Wikipedia and GTABase, the store details from the Steam, Epic, PlayStation and Xbox store pages, and the mission details from GTABase and Grand Theft Wiki. The episodes are our own playthrough, with no story spoilers beyond what each one shows.

Why play Vice City before GTA 6?

Because GTA 6 has gone back to the same city. Rockstar describes GTA 6 as a return to modern-day Vice City, and some names are coming back with it, such as the radio station Flash FM. The original is where most of that started: Tommy Vercetti, the pastel suits, the neon and a soundtrack of real 1980s hits.

It was a huge hit in its day, too. GGRecon notes that NPD's sales figures put Vice City at number one in the US for 2002, ahead of Madden and GTA III.

And if you're on PC, there's a practical reason. Rockstar hasn't announced GTA 6 for PC (our GTA 6 on PC guide has the details), so Vice City is the easiest way to spend time in the city on a computer right now.

Episode 1: In the Beginning

The game opens in 1986, when Sonny Forelli sends Tommy Vercetti from Liberty City to Vice City. GTABase sums up what happens next: a drug deal with the Vance brothers is ambushed, Tommy escapes, and he drives to the Ocean View Hotel. The opening isn't counted as a mission by the game, so once the cutscene ends you're free to explore before heading to the hotel.

Episode 2: An Old Friend and The Party

In An Old Friend, Sonny Forelli phones Tommy at the hotel, and afterwards Tommy heads to his lawyer, Ken Rosenberg. In The Party, Rosenberg points him at the retired Colonel Juan Cortez, who Grand Theft Wiki says helped set up the deal. Tommy picks up new clothes at Rafael's and goes to Cortez's yacht party, where GTABase says he's introduced to Ricardo Diaz. Then he drives Mercedes Cortez to the Pole Position Club. Grand Theft Wiki lists the reward as $100 and the Soiree outfit.

How can you play Vice City today?

The version on sale now is Grand Theft Auto: Vice City – The Definitive Edition, the 2021 remaster. GTABase lists it for PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One and Nintendo Switch from 11 November 2021. It was developed by Rockstar and adapted by Grove Street Games, the console store pages say.

  • On PC: Vice City's Steam page lists it as included with GTA+, Rockstar's subscription. On the Epic Games Store, it's only sold as part of The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition.
  • On PlayStation and Xbox: it's part of The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition, and Xbox's store page also lists the Trilogy as included with GTA+.

It won't need a powerful PC. Steam's minimum specs are Windows 10, an Intel Core i5-6600K or AMD FX-6300, 8 GB of RAM, an Nvidia GeForce GTX 760 or AMD Radeon R9 280, and 10 GB of free space.

MYTH

"Vice City in GTA 6 is the same map as the old game." It isn't. GTA 6's Vice City is a new, modern-day version in the HD universe, and it's part of a much bigger state, Leonida. Our GTA timeline guide explains how the two Vice Cities fit.
